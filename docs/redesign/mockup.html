HandStrudel — proposed structure

The change isn't the paint, it's the bones. Four real destinations via a tab bar instead of one linear flow + a junk-drawer sheet. Play gets a persistent control deck. Loops becomes a first-class place for the capture→layer→arrange flow. Sounds holds presets + store. Tune is the small set you change mid-session; everything power-user folds under Advanced.

Tune (the dismantled settings sheet). Grouped, not dumped: Harmony (key/scale/chords), Feel (tempo + effects), Mode options (only the active mode's controls), Look (filter + hand theme). The cockpit — mapping, 2nd drums, sequencer, code, recording — sits under Advanced.

The settings sheet, dismantled

The single endless scroll is the core structural flaw. It's broken into grouped surfaces with real homes — nothing is lost, but players see only what they need.
